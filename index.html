Download the latest version (0.1.1).

Download for Mac Download for Windows

The Mac file is for Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or newer).

If your computer shows a warning

Mac: open the downloaded file, drag Church Portal to Applications, then right-click the app and choose Open, and confirm Open again.

Windows: if SmartScreen appears, click More info, then Run anyway.

Before installing an update, open Church Portal and use Back up data from the menu. Your church data is kept when you update.
